Lily, a sheep, investigates the murder of her shepherd with the help of her friends Sebastian, Mopple, and the rest of her flock. The film explores a wide variety of interesting concepts and totally does justice to the premise of "what do animals think?" by exploring every aspect of their nature thoroughly, how they feel about death, how they feel about belonging, etc. It engages the viewer with many thoughts and lets us draw sharp analogies with the human world.
